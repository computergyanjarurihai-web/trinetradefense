Research foundation · Project MUSSTI

Multi-Sensor Surveillance Using Sensor Fusion

TRINETRA is built on a research programme led by Dr. Kumar Krishen, a former NASA scientist. Its goal is to remotely sense stationary and moving objects for national safety and security, even when they are camouflaged, hidden under forest or shrubs, or in terrain that people cannot reach.

The origin US Patent 5,005,147

"Method and Apparatus for Sensor Fusion", granted in 1991 to the United States as represented by the NASA Administrator. Inventors: Kumar Krishen, Scott W. Shaw and Rui J. P. de Figueiredo. The method fuses optical and radar data to reconstruct the shape of an unknown surface at a distance.

  • Physics-based fusion

    Algorithms based on how waves scatter and emit across the electromagnetic spectrum combine data from many sensors.

  • More information, less uncertainty

    Combined sensors tell us more about a scene than any single sensor, which improves accuracy.

  • Vision in bad conditions

    Works at night and through cloud, fog, drizzle and light rain or snow. Weather readings are used to fine-tune detection.

ALGORITHM · DKOS

Document Known Objects & Scenes

Ground data and photos of known situations teach the system attributes such as size, shape, signature strength, surroundings, people and weapons.

ALGORITHM · IUOS

Identify Unknown Objects & Scenes

Live sensor data is compared with the known library. A deep-learning decision module answers what it is, when it appeared and where it is.

METHODS

Detection toolkit

Physics-based models, deep learning and neural networks, fuzzy logic, and wavelet analysis for compression, denoising and feature extraction.

DISPLAY

Augmented reality for decision makers

Identified objects are shown at their location in real video or photo surroundings, so commanders see the situation at a glance.

Three layers, one eye

Every layer watches. TRINETRA connects them.

Each layer covers the blind spots of the others. Space gives the wide view, drones give the close look, and ground sensors give round-the-clock watch.

How it works

From signal to action in three steps

01 / Sense

Detect

All three layers collect data continuously. Edge AI filters out noise like animals, weather and routine traffic.

On-device processing
02 / Analyse

Recognise

The central AI cross-checks a signal across layers. A ground alert is confirmed by drone or satellite before it is raised.

Multi-layer verification
03 / Alert

Respond

Verified threats reach the right officer with location, image and threat level, on screen or on mobile.
