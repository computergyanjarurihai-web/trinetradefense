Three layers, one eye

Every layer watches. TRINETRA connects them.

Each layer covers the blind spots of the others. Space gives the wide view, drones give the close look, and ground sensors give round-the-clock watch.

Orbit500 km +

Space layer

  • Satellite imagery for borders, coastlines and forests
  • AI change detection: new tracks, structures, vessels
  • Wide-area watch across thousands of square kilometres
Aerial100 m – 5 km

Drone layer

  • Autonomous drone patrols on alert or on schedule
  • Thermal and night vision for low-light conditions
  • Live video to the command centre for confirmation
Ground0 – 100 m

Sensor layer

  • Smart cameras, radar, seismic and acoustic sensors
  • Edge AI on the device works without internet
  • Solar-powered units for remote and rural posts
How it works

From signal to action in three steps

01 / Sense

Detect

All three layers collect data continuously. Edge AI filters out noise like animals, weather and routine traffic.

On-device processing
02 / Analyse

Recognise

The central AI cross-checks a signal across layers. A ground alert is confirmed by drone or satellite before it is raised.

Multi-layer verification
03 / Alert

Respond

Verified threats reach the right officer with location, image and threat level, on screen or on mobile.

Real-time alerting
